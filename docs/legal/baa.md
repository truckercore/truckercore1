# Business Associate Agreement (BAA)

Only applicable if Customer is a Covered Entity or Business Associate under HIPAA and uploads PHI to TruckerCore.

1. Definitions: PHI, Covered Entity, Business Associate, etc.
2. Permitted Uses and Disclosures: solely to perform services.
3. Safeguards: administrative/physical/technical per 45 CFR §164.308-.312.
4. Subcontractors: ensure BA-level protections via written contracts.
5. Breach Notification: without unreasonable delay (≤ 10 business days) with required details.
6. Access/Amendment/Accounting: assist Covered Entity as required.
7. Termination: return/destroy PHI; infeasible case—extend protections and limit use.
8. Miscellaneous: Indemnity per MSA; survival.
