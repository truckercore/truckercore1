# Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

This Data Processing Addendum (“DPA”) forms part of the Master Services Agreement (“Agreement”) between Customer and TruckerCore.

1. Definitions
- “Controller”, “Processor”, “Personal Data”, “Processing” have the meanings in GDPR.
- “CCPA” includes the CPRA amendments.
- “Customer Data” means data provided by or on behalf of Customer.

2. Roles
- Customer is Controller; TruckerCore is Processor (and Service Provider under CCPA).

3. Processing Details
- Subject matter: Provision of TruckerCore platform.
- Duration: Term of Agreement.
- Nature/purpose: Hosting, analytics, support, fraud prevention.
- Data subjects: Customer employees, contractors, drivers, end users.
- Categories: Identification, contact, telemetry (coarse), transactional.

4. Processor Obligations
- Process only on documented instructions from Customer.
- Confidentiality; background-checked personnel.
- Security: administrative, technical, physical measures (see Security Overview).
- Subprocessors: maintain list; notify of changes; allow objection per Agreement.
- Data Subject Requests: assist Customer.
- Breach Notification: without undue delay; content per Art. 33/34 GDPR.

5. CCPA
- Service Provider: no sale or sharing of personal information.
- Use limited to services; assist with consumer rights requests.

6. International Transfers
- SCCs Module 2 (Controller → Processor) incorporated by reference.
- Data Residency: see Data Residency Statement.

7. Deletion/Return
- On termination, return or delete Customer Personal Data; backups per retention.

8. Audits
- Provide audit reports or summaries; reasonable assistance; SOC 2 when available.

9. Miscellaneous
- Conflicts: DPA prevails over Agreement to extent of conflict.
- Signatures: This DPA is effective on Agreement effective date.
