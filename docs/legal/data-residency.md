# Data Residency Statement

Primary processing region: United States (AWS region: us-east-1 / us-west-2).
- Databases: US-based.
- Backups: US-based encrypted backups with 30-day retention by default.
- Telemetry: aggregated and anonymized where feasible; raw telemetry retention 14–30 days.
- International transfers: governed by SCCs; additional safeguards (encryption in transit/at rest, access controls).
- Upon request and availability, EU-region hosting may be offered under a separate order.
