# Pilot Plan (10–20 Locations, 12 Weeks)

## Goals
- +10% fuel gallons or +5% with ROI > 3x promo cost.
- Parking freshness ≥ 85%; Redemption approval > 80%.

## KPIs
- Fuel gallons uplift vs baseline
- Promo funnel: impressions → saves → scans → approvals → gallons (est)
- Parking confidence/freshness
- SSO uptime (if enabled)

## Timeline
- Week 1: Setup (POIs, promos, SSO optional), baseline snapshot
- Weeks 2–11: Run promos, monitor KPIs, weekly report
- Week 12: Final report, ROI summary, enterprise proposal

## Success Criteria
- Hit KPI thresholds; executive summary deck delivered; expansion pricing approved.
