# Privacy & Data Rights

## Retention
- Telemetry (raw): 14–30 days
- Aggregates: 12–24 months
- Audit logs: 24 months
- Billing records: 7 years

## Export
- Users can export personal data (JSON/CSV) from Profile → Privacy → Export.
- Exports are delivered via email link and expire in 24 hours.

## Delete
- Users can request account deletion from Profile → Privacy → Delete.
- Accounts are soft‑deleted for 30 days, then purged. Certain financial records may be retained per law.

## Operator/Fleet Admin
- Org‑wide exports (drivers, loads, promos) are available under Admin → Data → Export.
