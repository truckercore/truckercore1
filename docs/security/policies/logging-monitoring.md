# Logging & Monitoring Policy (Skeleton)

Purpose: Ensure sufficient telemetry to detect, alert, and investigate incidents.

Controls:
- Centralized logs with access controls; retention at least 12–24 months where applicable
- Alert thresholds for critical services; dashboards for health (SSO/SCIM, uptime)
- Incident metrics tracked (MTTA/MTTR)
- PII-aware summaries and masking in logs

Evidence: dashboards, alert rules, sample logs, weekly/quarterly reports.
