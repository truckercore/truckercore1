# Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery (BCDR) Policy (Skeleton)

Purpose: Provide continuity of operations through backups, restores, and failover.

Controls:
- Daily automated backups; RTO=4h, RPO=24h
- Annual restore drill with validation checklist
- Failover plan documented; maintenance and communication procedures
- Post-restore monitoring and backfill as required

Evidence: backup reports, restore drill results, failover runbooks.
