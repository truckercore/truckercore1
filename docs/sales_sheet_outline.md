# TruckerCore Sales Sheet – Design-Ready Outline (PDF 1–2 pages)

This document is a client-ready content and layout outline for a branded, single- or double-page PDF sales sheet. It includes final copy blocks, layout structure, visuals guidance, and a pricing comparison table designers can drop in.

Meta
- Brand: TruckerCore
- Product tagline: "Driver Funnel + Data Engine for Truck Stops"
- Target audience: Truck stop operators (corporate, regional, location managers)
- Format: PDF (1–2 pages)
- Voice: Confident, data-driven, operator-focused

---

Page 1 — Core Pitch + Pricing + Visuals

1. Brand Header
   - TruckerCore logo (top-left)
   - Tagline: Driver Funnel + Data Engine for Truck Stops
   - Optional: "Works with" partner strip (if approved): Comdata / EFS / WEX logos (small, muted)

2. Hero Visual (split or collage)
   - Left: Driver app map view with featured promotions cards (brand chips, badges, "Tap to Redeem" QR)
   - Right: Operator Portal analytics snapshot (promo funnel chart and parking dashboard tile)
   - Alt text (for accessibility notes): Driver app showing nearby promos; Operator Portal with analytics widgets

3. Lead Statement (body copy)
   TruckerCore is the driver’s daily tool (navigation, load management, compliance). By integrating your truck stop network, we funnel millions of driver touchpoints directly to your locations and give your corporate team hard ROI data.

4. Three-Tier Pricing Table (Free / Premium / Enterprise)

   Columns: Free | Premium | Enterprise

   Rows (✓ = included, — = not included):
   - Listing on Driver Map: ✓ | ✓ | ✓
   - Promotions & QR Checkout: — | ✓ (unlimited) | ✓ (unlimited + fleet-only)
   - Featured Placement: — | ✓ | ✓ (sponsored tiers)
   - Live Parking Updates: Crowd only | Operator control + IoT | Operator + IoT + predictive
   - Fuel Price Management: Manual | Scheduled + competitiveness | Scheduled + corporate playbooks
   - Analytics: Basic | ROI dashboards + exports | Executive forecasting + benchmarking
   - Integrations: — | POS webhooks | POS adaptors, SSO, data feeds
   - Support: Email (72h) | Business (24–48h) | 24/7, 99.9% SLA
   - Price: Free | 199–499 / month / stop | Custom (chain-wide)

   Notes for designer:
   - Use high-contrast column headers and subtle row dividers.
   - Emphasize Premium column (slight highlight/brand tint).
   - Include footnote: Pricing varies by market and volume.

5. Visuals Section (side-by-side callouts)
   A. "Win the Driver" (Driver App)
   - Mock elements: Promos list with brand badges; “Tap to Redeem” QR; map pin highlight
   - Callout bullets: Featured placement, loyalty badges, live parking

   B. "Prove the ROI" (Operator Portal)
   - Mock elements: Promo funnel (Impressions → Saves → Scans → Approved → Revenue)
   - Mock chart: Fuel competitiveness vs nearby median
   - Mock tile: Parking heatmap with forecast badge “80% by 8:15 pm Fridays”

6. Key Analytics Callouts (captioned badges)
   - Fuel & Revenue: “5¢ cheaper vs median → +12% gallons (Fri–Sun)”
   - Promotions: “10¢ off diesel drove +18% gallons this weekend”
   - Parking & Amenities: “Drivers with reservations spent $22 more in-store”
   - Reputation: Reviews dashboard; trending issues and responses

7. Security & Operations Badges (icon row)
   - Short-lived QR tokens, one-time nonce, anti-fraud velocity checks
   - Role-based access; audit logs; POS webhooks available

8. ROI Pitch Block (short paragraph + bullets)
   Heading: TruckerCore = Driver Funnel + Data Engine
   Bullets:
   - More drivers stopping → fleet integration + featured placement
   - More money per stop → reservations, upsells, promos
   - Lower operating costs → predictive staffing, automated reporting
   - Better corporate visibility → benchmarking, competitive share

9. CTA Footer
   - Headline: Next Step: Pilot a 3‑month trial with 10–20 locations
   - Support copy: Track parking, fuel, and promo ROI; compare traffic vs competitor exits
   - Contact: sales@truckercore.com | truckercore.com/operators
   - CTA button (bright/primary): "Start Pilot Consultation"

Design Guidance

Palette & Imagery
- Use brand primaries; high-contrast CTA buttons
- Photography: night-lit forecourt, driver with phone, cashier scanning QR

Icons
- Fuel pump, QR, map pin, chart/arrow-up, shield (security), badge/star (reviews)

Layout Tips
- Page 1: Value prop + pricing table + driver/portal visuals
- Page 2 (optional): Analytics deep-dive with three mini-charts and enterprise benefits
- Keep ample whitespace; emphasize scannability

Logos
- TruckerCore logo top-left
- Optional “Works with” strip (Comdata/EFS/WEX), pending approval

Accessibility Notes (for design)
- Ensure sufficient color contrast for text on brand tints
- Provide alt text for key visuals in digital version
- Avoid text baked into images when possible

Pricing Table — Plain Text (copy-ready)

Free Listing (baseline)
- Basic Location Presence: listed on driver map (address, amenities)
- Crowdsourced Parking Signals (no operator control)
- Community Reviews (view only)
- Manual Updates Only (no real-time)
- Note: Good for visibility; Premium operators rank higher and look better

Premium Operator (per-location subscription)
- Price: 199–499 / month / stop
- Tools to Attract More Drivers:
  - Unlimited promotions (fuel, food, services)
  - QR & Code redemption for faster checkout
  - Featured placement in driver app (above non-premium)
  - Real-time Parking dashboard (Open/Some/Full)
- Analytics That Prove ROI:
  - Parking utilization heatmaps
  - Fuel price competitiveness (10–50 mi radius)
  - Promo funnel (view → save → redeem)
  - Weekly ROI summary (“+$3,240 fuel revenue last week”)
- Operational Efficiency:
  - Automated receipts to fleets/drivers
  - Staff role logins (corporate, regional, location)
  - Respond to reviews from Operator Portal

Enterprise Corporate Partnership (chain-wide)
- Custom pricing (contact sales)
- National Scale:
  - White-label portal (“Powered by TruckerCore”), SSO, enterprise roles
  - Corporate analytics: benchmarking, market share vs competitors
- Fleet Integration:
  - Real-time fuel/parking in fleet dispatch
  - Fleet-only promos; digital receipts API
- Data & Insights:
  - Predictive parking forecasts
  - Driver cohort retention (7/30/90 day)
  - Competitive heatmaps
- Partnership Perks:
  - Sponsored placement in driver app
  - Shared revenue: premium parking, ad targeting
  - Dedicated account manager, 99.9% SLA
