# Alerting and Incident Response

Severity Model
- Critical (P0): page on-call immediately; engage incident commander; update status; postmortem required.
- High (P1): page during business hours or on-call as per rotation; ticket + follow-up.
- Medium (P2): create ticket; prioritize in sprint.
- Low (P3): backlog; monitor.

Rotations
- Maintain on-call schedule and escalation path in ops tooling.
